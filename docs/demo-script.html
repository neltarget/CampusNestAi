Recording Tips

  • Use the actual live app — investors can tell the difference
  • Move the mouse naturally — don't rush, let each screen breathe for 2–3 seconds
  • Pause briefly at the pipeline animation — that's your "wow" moment
  • Keep the voiceover calm and confident — not salesy, more "look at this"
  • Background music: soft ambient, no lyrics — it should support, not compete
  • Export at 1080p minimum — blurry screen recordings kill credibility
